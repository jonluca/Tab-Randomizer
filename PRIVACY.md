# Tab Randomizer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Tab Randomizer is a browser extension developed by JonLuca DeCaro. When the user clicks its toolbar button, it selects an open tab at random and focuses that tab and its browser window.

## Data the extension accesses

The browser's `tabs` permission makes open-tab metadata available to the extension. Tab Randomizer uses tab and window identifiers only to choose and focus a random candidate. It does not inspect page content or use tab URLs or titles for profiling, tracking, or any other purpose.

## Collection, storage, and sharing

All selection happens locally. Tab Randomizer does not collect, upload, store, sell, or share open-tab metadata, browsing history, page content, form data, credentials, communications, or other personal information. It makes no external network requests and uses no analytics or advertising services.

## Retention and user control

The extension retains no user data. Removing it ends its access immediately.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be reflected in this document and its updated date. Questions may be sent to [chrome@jonlu.ca](mailto:chrome@jonlu.ca).
